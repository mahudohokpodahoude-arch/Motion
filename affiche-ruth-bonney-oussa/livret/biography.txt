BIOGRAPHY OF THE LATE MRS. RUTH BONNEY OUSSA
(alias Da Adzo)
Mrs. Ruth Bonney Oussa, affectionately known as Da Adzo, was born on December 30, 1963. She was the beloved daughter of the late Mr. Thomas Komla Adzrakutse (from Alavanyo Abehenease) and the late Mrs. Martha Yawa Adzinai (from Gbi Wegbe), both of blessed and cherished memory.
Madam Ruth was raised in a home grounded in faith, discipline, and a strong work ethic. She developed a firm moral character at a young age. These core principles guided and honored her throughout her entire life. She was devoted wife, loving mother, affectionate grandmother, sister, aunt, and loyal friend, she built alongside her husband a home filled with warmth, love, and hospitality, where every visitor found refuge. Eleven years ago, following the passing of her beloved husband, she courageously carried the family mantle alone with admirable strength and dignity.
She was a woman of conviction, hard work, and enterprise, Da Adzo distinguished herself in business through diligence, resilience, and sharp acumen. Her dedication allowed her not only to provide honorably for her family, but also to make a lasting impact on her community through her generosity and leadership by example.
Above all, Da Adzo was a fervent woman of prayer. Firmly believing that a family’s destiny is built on its knees, she daily lifted her loved ones before the Lord—both in the quiet of her room and in the heart of her home. Her prayers remain the most precious legacy she leaves behind.
During the eleven years following her husband's departure, she never faltered. Leaning with unwavering faith upon the Lord, she dedicated herself entirely to the upbringing, education, and spiritual growth of her children. Correcting with tenderness, encouraging with love, and exhorting them toward integrity, she taught them to forgive and walk in truth. Today, her children acknowledge that all they are is by God's grace and the prayers of this exceptional mother.
A woman of profound grace and faith, she devoted her life to serving others. Remembered for her natural kindness and gentle strength, her memory will remain forever etched in the hearts of all who knew her.
She left behind five children whom she cherished, carried in prayer, and led in the ways of the Lord: Huldah Orounla, Michèle Oussa, Godwill Oussa, Anne Marie Oussa, and Jean Claude Oussa. Though deeply grieved by this great loss, they give thanks to God for the privilege of having been guided by such a mother and commit to upholding her legacy of faith and love.
## Her Grandchildren
A loving and protective grandmother, she surrounded her grandchildren with blessings and prayers, instilling in them the fear of God and love for family. They remain her pride and the continuation of her spiritual heritage.
## Her Siblings
She is also mourned by her brothers and sisters: Theresa Videkpui, Noah Videkpui, Mr. Yohanes Mawusi Adzrakutse, Pastor Emmanuel Kosi Adzrakutse, Mr. David Adzei, Mrs. Florence Adzei-Awuda, and Sister Abuya, as well as her nephews, nieces, and extended family.
## REST IN PEACE
Mrs. Ruth Bonney Oussa has fought the good fight, finished the race, and kept the faith. The crown of righteousness now awaits her. May she rest peacefully in the arms of the Lord whom she loved and served so faithfully.
May her gentle and peaceful soul rest in divine light until the day of resurrection.
